Answer these five questions about e-Parimap:
Q1) Does the public search return the certificate’s contents, or does it return whether the certificate is valid today? Take a screenshot of what it returns.
ANS.)The public e-Parimap certificate-search facility could not be tested because the e-Parimap portal is currently inaccessible. Therefore, it was not possible to determine whether the public search returns the complete certificate contents or only the certificate's current validity status.

Q2)Does it say anywhere the date on which the check was made?
ANS)No, the certificate does not mention the date on which the actual check or testing was conducted. While the text notes that an evaluation report was submitted by the prescribed authority, the exact date of that physical inspection is omitted entirely.  The date appearing below the signature (18.03.2026) is something else entirely; it is simply the digital signature timestamp recording when the official signed and issued the certificate, rather than when the physical testing took place. 

Q3)Is there any indication that the holder is told before a certificate expires?
ANS)NO reminder or notification is sent to the certificate holder before the certificate expires.The holder is therefore required to keep track of the certificate’s validity/expiry date and arrange for the required re-verification or renewal.

Q4)Does it cover all instrument types, or only some?
ANS)The West Bengal e-Parimap portal is currently not working/inaccessible. However, various West Bengal certificates relating to different weighing and measuring instruments are available on the Government of India's Legal Metrology certificate database: "https://lm.doca.gov.in/pcr/certificates". Based on the certificates available on this portal, multiple types of weighing/measuring instruments are covered. Since the e-Parimap portal is currently inaccessible, it cannot be independently confirmed whether ALL instrument types are covered by the e-Parimap verification system.

Q5)Does any other State have something similar? Check Karnataka, Tamil Nadu and Maharashtra again with this specific question in mind.
ANS)Yes. Karnataka, Tamil Nadu and Maharashtra have similar online Legal Metrology facilities.Their respective online portals are accessible, allowing the public to search/check different certificates and related information.

**Karnataka:** The Legal Metrology online portal is accessible and allows users to search/check certificates for weighing and measuring instruments. No notification/reminder is sent to the certificate holder before the verification expires. The system covers different types of weighing and measuring instruments.

**Tamil Nadu:** The Legal Metrology online portal is accessible and allows users to search/check certificates for weighing and measuring instruments. No notification/reminder is sent to the certificate holder before the verification expires. The system covers different types of weighing and measuring instruments.

**Maharashtra:** The Legal Metrology online portal is accessible and allows users to search/check certificates for weighing and measuring instruments. No notification/reminder is sent to the certificate holder before the verification expires. The system covers different types of weighing and measuring instruments.

**Comparison with West Bengal:** Unlike these three States, the West Bengal e-Parimap portal is currently inaccessible. Therefore, the West Bengal system could not be directly tested. However, various West Bengal certificates are available through the Government of India's Legal Metrology certificate database.
