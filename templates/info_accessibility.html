Accessibility statement

We aim for WCAG 2.1 level AA, the level the Guidelines for Indian Government Websites and Apps (GIGW 3.0) require.

What you can do on this site

  • Change the text size with the A-, A and A+ buttons at the top, or with your browser's zoom.
  • Switch to high contrast with the button at the top.
  • Use every page with a keyboard alone. "Skip to main content" is the first link on each page.
  • Read every status as a word and a symbol, not by colour alone.
  • Check a certificate with JavaScript turned off.

Known limits

  • The site is in English only. A Hindi version is planned.
  • This statement has not been confirmed by an independent accessibility audit.
