Privacy

What this prototype stores

  • Checking a certificate: nothing about you is saved in the database. Like any website, the server and our hosting provider keep short-lived logs of the pages requested, and the server counts requests per IP address for a few minutes to stop abuse.
  • Forms: when you open a form, your browser gets a cookie holding a random token. It only proves the form came from this site.
  • Public reports: the serial number and the text you type, with the time and a reference number. We ask you not to include personal details.
  • Officers: username, a hashed password, and an audit record of actions taken.
  • Your settings: text size and contrast are remembered only in your own browser.

No advertising or tracking. Certificate data in this prototype is test data, not real people's records.
