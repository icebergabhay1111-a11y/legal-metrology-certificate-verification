Offline certificate checker

For weighbridges, mandis and checkposts with no signal. Open this page once while online; after that it works with no network. It checks that the certificate's QR code was signed by Sahi Daam and has not been altered.

Check a QR code

Loading the checker…

Most phone cameras can copy a QR code's text. It starts with the certificate's web address.

What an offline check can and cannot tell you

  • It can tell you the QR code was issued by this system and that none of its details (ID, serial number, owner, type, dates, officer) has been changed.
  • It works out expiry from your phone's date. If the phone's date is wrong, so is that result.
  • It cannot tell you whether the certificate was revoked after it was printed. Check online when you have signal.
  • It needs a recent browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox from 2025 on). Camera scanning works where the browser supports it; pasting works everywhere.
